Fly Brain, Calcium Imaging

Neurons glow green when they fire and fade slowly as calcium clears. Every spike lands on the sixteenth-note grid and becomes a note. Click any cell to make it fire.

Press start, then click cells to fire them.Take: empty

The take records up to 32 bars. Export gives a zip holding a 2-track .mid: melody plus bass and chords that follow what the brain just played. Synthetic wiring, not real FlyWire data.

An interactive art toy — every neuron here is simulated. All audio and visuals run locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored. Wiring is synthetic, not real FlyWire data. Your exported MIDI takes are yours to keep and use however you like. Free to share and remix (MIT).

Credits & inspiration: typefaces via Google Fonts (Familjen Grotesk, Hanken Grotesk, JetBrains Mono). Concept inspired by the FlyWire whole-brain connectome (Princeton / Cambridge / Janelia) and whole-brain calcium imaging in behaving Drosophila (Aimon et al., PLOS Biology 2019). No real neural data is used or displayed.
